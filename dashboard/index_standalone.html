The honest trade-off vs. the strongest baseline

Against the mobility-aware baseline, NHI is a genuine trade, not a clean win:

MetricNHI dynamicBaselineVerdict

SoH spread (pedestrian)0.02720.0320NHI better

NHI trades ~4–5 extra migrations per 60 placements for statistically significant improvements in battery-wear fairness — a dimension the mobility baseline ignores entirely.

A conceptual correction found mid-study

Rewarding high SoH alone sends every task to the healthiest node — wearing it down fastest and widening fleet divergence, the opposite of the goal. Measured directly: an 8-metric NHI had worse SoH spread (0.0350) than the mobility baseline (0.0280). The fix was a ninth term — wear balance, the normalised inverse of work already assigned — added because the mechanism demanded it, not because it improved a number after the fact.
